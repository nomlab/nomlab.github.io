In my first month, I can’t remember some of NomLab’s members’ name. Don’t worry, I can remember everyone now. 

Before coming here, I was very nervous, but everyone in NomLab is very kind to me such as when if I don’t understand something 
and want to ask someone, there will be more than 2 persons answer me. That’s very surprising.

My first trip here was in Okayama, I went to Okayama castle and Korakuen garden with Momay and Urng on 3rd June. 
That day was shiny, but not too hot for us. 

When we walked in the garden, we saw Sakura tree. So, I took a photo and posted
 in my Facebook with caption “Hello, It’s me, Sakura without flower!”. No one thought it was Sakura tree at all 555
